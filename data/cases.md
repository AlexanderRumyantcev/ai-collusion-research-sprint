# Cases and the graph's answer

Generated by `data/build_cases.py` from `tests/results/items.csv` and
`tests/results/cases.csv`, which `npm test` writes. Do not edit by hand.

The graph is a rule engine: its answer follows from the labels, so the
tables show how the method behaves on this labelling, not whether the
labelling is correct. Labels were assigned by one rater (the author).

## 1. Evidence types from published work

Every row of the dataset, in the order the test output produces them,
with no rows dropped. `scored` rows carry the element they bear on in
the report and the weight the graph gives them; `excluded` rows were
read and deliberately left unscored, and the last column says why.
`weight` is the ordinal weight of the item (4 direct and reliable,
3 direct and doubtful, 2 circumstantial and reliable, 1 circumstantial
and doubtful, 0 unreliable); a negative weight is exculpatory.

## 2. Case configurations and the standard reached

A configuration is a set of evidence available in one case. The
standard reached is the highest rung of the ladder the evidence meets:
reasonable suspicion, probable cause, substantial evidence,
preponderance of the evidence, clear and convincing evidence, beyond a
reasonable doubt. `f` items are the demo's preset items about a
hypothetical episode, not published data.
